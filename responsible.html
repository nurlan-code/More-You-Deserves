Our Commitment to Responsible Gaming

At GoldRush Social, we are committed to providing a safe and enjoyable gaming environment. While our platform operates in a demo mode with no real money involved, we recognize the importance of responsible gaming practices and the potential risks associated with gaming behavior.

We are dedicated to promoting responsible gaming and providing resources to help our users maintain a healthy relationship with gaming.

What is Responsible Gaming?

Responsible gaming means enjoying our platform as a form of entertainment while maintaining control over your gaming activities. It involves:

  • Playing for fun and entertainment, not as a way to make money or escape problems
  • Setting limits on time and engagement
  • Recognizing when gaming may be affecting your daily life
  • Knowing that virtual coins have no real-world value
  • Understanding that outcomes are random and based on chance

Setting Limits

Even though GoldRush Social is a free-to-play platform, we encourage you to set personal limits for your gaming sessions:

  • Time Limits: Set a timer or schedule breaks during your gaming sessions
  • Session Limits: Decide in advance how long you want to play and stick to it
  • Frequency Limits: Take regular breaks between gaming sessions

Recognizing Problem Gaming

It's important to be aware of the signs that gaming may be becoming problematic. If you notice any of these signs, we encourage you to seek help:

  • Gaming for longer than intended
  • Feeling restless or irritable when not gaming
  • Gaming as a way to escape from problems or relieve feelings of helplessness or anxiety
  • Trying to cut back or stop gaming but being unable to do so
  • Neglecting work, school, or family responsibilities due to gaming
  • Lying to family members or others about the extent of your gaming
  • Jeopardizing relationships, jobs, or opportunities because of gaming

Remember: Even though no real money is involved, excessive gaming can still impact your daily life and well-being.

Age Restrictions

You must be at least 18 years old to use GoldRush Social. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 18. If we discover that we have collected information from a minor, we will delete that information immediately.

Self-Exclusion

If you feel that you need to take a break from gaming, you can:

  • Delete your account through your account settings
  • Contact our support team for assistance with account management
  • Use browser extensions or parental control software to limit access to gaming sites

If you choose to delete your account, all your game progress, virtual coins, and account data will be permanently removed.
